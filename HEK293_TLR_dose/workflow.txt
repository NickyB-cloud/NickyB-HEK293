HEK293 TLR REPORTER: DOSE-RESPONSE FIGURES
==========================================

What this folder does
---------------------
Plots HEK293 TLR4/MD-2 reporter cell (NF-kB -> secreted alkaline
phosphatase, read by absorbance) responses to purified lipid A fractions
from several bacterial strains, separated by thin-layer chromatography Rf
band, at two doses, in human and mouse TLR4 reporter lines.


Input: a tidy table made BY HAND
--------------------------------
In the original workflow the tidy table was typed/assembled by hand in a
spreadsheet from the plate-reader output and saved as CSV (no parsing
script). Make yours with exactly these columns, one row per replicate well:

  Species     human | mouse           (which reporter cell line)
  Wavelength  e.g. 620                (absorbance read wavelength, nm)
  Strain      strain ID, e.g. 381, 1587, 1773; positive control = Pos_cont.
  Rf          Rf band of the fraction, e.g. 0.68 (blank for Pos_cont.)
  Dose        1ug | 10ug              (per mL; blank is read as 1ug)
  Replicate   1, 2, 3 ...
  FoldChange  the well's absorbance relative to the unstimulated
              (negative-control) wells of the same plate, calculated in the
              spreadsheet (the scripts do not recalculate it)

Example rows:
  Species,Wavelength,Strain,Rf,Dose,Replicate,FoldChange
  mouse,620,1587,0.68,10ug,1,3.19
  human,620,Pos_cont.,,1ug,1,20.4

Rf values are grouped per strain after rounding to 2 decimals, so 0.757
and 0.76 land on the same band.


Scripts
-------
HEK293_figures_stacked_GitHub.py   the composite figures (2-page PDF)
    Figure 1: all strains per species, human panel above mouse panel.
    Figure 2: human vs mouse, one panel per strain.
HEK293_exploratory_GitHub.py       exploratory multi-page PDF with the same
    data plotted three ways (all strains per species; human vs mouse per
    strain; x-axis grouped by strain then Rf).

Both: points = replicate wells on a log2 axis (tick labels in fold
change); marker = geometric mean +/- geometric SEM; positive control
drawn to the left of an axis break.
Stats: one-way ANOVA + Tukey HSD on log2 fold change (between Rf bands
within a strain; human vs mouse within strain x Rf).
Stars (Tukey-adjusted): * P<0.05, ** P<0.01, *** P<0.001.


Usage
-----
    python HEK293_figures_stacked_GitHub.py HEK_tidy_dose.csv
        -> HEK293_figures_stacked.pdf
    python HEK293_exploratory_GitHub.py HEK_tidy_dose.csv
        -> HEK293_exploratory_<timestamp>.pdf
(Without an argument both scripts look for HEK_tidy_dose.csv in the
current folder.)


What to change for your own data (marked "EDIT FOR YOUR DATA" in the code)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Strain IDs (PGIN_STRAINS / ECOLI_STRAINS), positive-control label,
  dose labels and colors in the Constants block.
- y-axis range (LOG2_YTICKS_LINEAR / LOG2_YLIM) if your responses go
  above 64-fold.
