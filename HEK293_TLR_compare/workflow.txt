HEK293 TLR REPORTER: LIPID A PREPARATION vs ITS Rf FRACTIONS
============================================================

What this folder does
---------------------
Compares one lipid A preparation as a whole ("mix", unfractionated)
against its individual Rf-band fractions in the HEK293 TLR4/MD-2 reporter
assay, in human and mouse reporter lines, at more than one timepoint
(e.g. 48 h and 72 h). Unlike ../HEK293_TLR_dose, the input here is raw
absorbance and the scripts calculate fold change themselves.


Scripts (run in this order)
---------------------------
1. parse_HEK293_mix_vs_purified_GitHub.py
   Plate-reader Excel export -> tidy table HEK293_mix_vs_purified_tidy.csv with
   columns Time, Species, Plate, Wavelength, Strain, Rf, Dose, Replicate,
   Absorbance (one row per well).

   Expected sheet layout (first sheet, no header row):
     column C  block label, e.g. "48H_1587_ole_mouse_620nm"
               (time "48H", species mouse|human, wavelength "620nm",
                optional plate "p1")
     columns D onward, same row: well labels, e.g.
               1587_mix  1587_68  1587_80  1587_86  pos_ctrl  neg_ctrl
               (<strain>_<Rf x 100 or "mix">, optional dose "1ug"/"10ug")
     rows below: one replicate per row, absorbance values
     an empty column-C row ends the block; stack as many blocks as needed.

2. HEK293_mix_vs_purified_GitHub.py
   Tidy table -> timestamped multi-page PDF:
     all strains per species x timepoint (mix vs each Rf band), and
     human vs mouse per strain at each timepoint.

Fold change: each well's absorbance / geometric mean of the "Neg"
(neg_ctrl) wells with the same Species x Time (x Plate).
Plots: points = replicate wells on a log2 axis (fold-change tick labels);
marker = geometric mean +/- geometric SEM.
Stats: one-way ANOVA + Tukey HSD on log2 fold change (mix vs each Rf band;
human vs mouse). Stars (Tukey-adjusted): * P<0.05, ** P<0.01, *** P<0.001.


Usage
-----
    python parse_HEK293_mix_vs_purified_GitHub.py your_plate_export.xlsx
        -> HEK293_mix_vs_purified_tidy.csv (+ .txt copy for reading)
    python HEK293_mix_vs_purified_GitHub.py HEK293_mix_vs_purified_tidy.csv
        -> HEK293_mix_vs_purified_<timestamp>.pdf


What to change for your own data (marked "EDIT FOR YOUR DATA" in the code)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Strain IDs, positive-control label and colors (Constants block of
  HEK293_mix_vs_purified_GitHub.py).
- XPOS_RF / RF_BAND_KEYS_STR / X_TICK_LABELS: the Rf bands of YOUR
  preparation and their x positions (the script hard-codes three bands).
